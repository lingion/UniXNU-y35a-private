Findings
1. XNU-4903 embeds panic header fields at offsets 0x14 and 0x18. In panic.raw they are eph_panic_log_offset=0xa9 and eph_panic_log_len=0xa37. The saved log begins at physical 0x859e00a9 and ends at 0x859e0ae0. It does not begin at the beginning of the pram allocation.
2. The earlier theory that physical 0x859e0000 lies inside [0xa0004000,0xb0004000) was false. It is outside that interval.
3. The earlier boot4 image used the generic DarwinBoot/XNU package path. That path has a zero_loop over its own owned window [0xa0004000,0xb0004000); this range does not overlap pram [0x859e0000,0x859f0000). It can explain why the diagnostic's kernel BSS globals were not valid at probe entry, but cannot by itself explain loss of pram text.
4. The reset-only probe used the documented warm-reset writes, performed no pram writes, and LK staged 0x11000 bytes via a copy to its download buffer. The staged result was byte-identical to the preceding boot4 capture. This establishes that reset-only plus staging did not further change the captured pram bytes.
5. 已证实的旧 readback staging 覆盖：manifest 将 early-fault record 写在 pram 偏移 0x40..0x1c0，随后写 `_BootArgs`、`_const_boot_args`、panic globals 等 128-byte 槽位，覆盖偏移约 0x40..0x640；`_excepstack` 写到 0x640..0x4640，`_intstack` 写到 0x4640..0xc640。旧有效 EPH 声明正文位于 [pram+0xa9, pram+0xae0)：前段 [0xa9,0x640) 被 early-fault/变量快照覆盖，后段 [0x640,0xae0) 被 exception stack 覆盖。这解释了旧 panic.raw 为何只从文件偏移 0x3c2（pram+0x382）显示 Calendar/Zone/launchd 等日志后段；原 panic message 不能从现有 dump 恢复。
6. reset-only probe 只执行两个 warm-reset 控制写，直接 LK stage；fastboot_stage 复制源到下载缓冲区后上传，未改写 PRAM。reset-only stage 与先前 boot4 capture 逐字节一致，说明这条 probe 没有进一步修改 capture 的源字节。
7. clean boot3（image SHA d7640a42...）直接 stage 后 EPH offset/len 均为零，panic 正文为空。LK log 顺序为 boot3 -> reset -> `oem y35a panic` -> `upload`，中间无第二次 XNU 启动。相较旧的两份有效 capture（offset 0xa9，len 0xa37），这是独立未解分叉；尚不能判断未写 header、nested panic、sync/flush 行为或 LK reset 路径影响，不能臆测其原因。
